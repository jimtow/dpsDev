ErgMate

ErgMate provides On-the-Water equivalent metrics for any Erg and settings based on an estimate of a rower's sustainable full-effort (FE) and cadence (SC).

ErgMate uses the rower's FE watts, 500m split time, and sustainable cadence (SC) to to calculate an On-the-Water equivalent distance per stroke and watts for a workout with any cadence and duration. ErgMate also includes a timer for tracking the cadence and workout duration.

Quick steps to "calibration": Enter your demographics (watts, 500m split), strap in, row a few strokes and estimate the maximum cadence that you can maintain for a 3-4 minute row with this erg and settings. Row to your 500m demographic split time and estimate your % of full ("race") effort.

Notes: Ergmate mechancial watts are adjusted using 25% human efficiency.
ErgMate is designed to be user-friendly and accessible
