ErgMate

On-the-water equivalent metrics for any erg
ErgMate provides on-the-water equivalent metrics for any erg and settings, based on an estimate of a rower's sustainable full-effort (FE) and cadence (SC).
ErgMate uses your FE watts, 500m split time, and sustainable cadence (SC) to calculate an on-the-water equivalent distance per stroke and watts for a workout at any cadence and duration. It also includes a timer for tracking cadence and workout duration, plus a virtual "cox" to help keep you on-cadence.
Quick steps to "calibration": Strap in, row a few strokes, and estimate the maximum cadence you can maintain for a 3-4 minute row with this erg and settings. Next, enter your demographics (watts, 500m split) and row to the 500m demographic split time while maintaining the maximum cadence and estimate the % of full ("race") effort you expended.

You're now good to go — any cadence, any distance, on this rowing machine and settings.
Notes: ErgMate mechanical watts are adjusted using 25% rower efficiency.
